# Sound credits

Almost every Palimpsest sound is synthesised from scratch by `tools/gen_sounds.py`. The Knocker's
door and window sounds are built from the field recordings below by `tools/gen_recorded.py`: cut
up, re-timed, pitched down, cleaned and put in a small room. The source files are in `tools/recordings/`.

All of them are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public
domain) on Freesound, so attribution isn't required, but it's listed here anyway, with thanks.
